Rentora

Property Listings • Search • Verified Rentals

A rental listings platform for Kenya built around verified homes, real photos and direct contact with landlords.

Visit Live Site ↗

Overview

House hunting in Kenya often means fake listings and agents who charge just to show a house. Rentora is built to cut that out: listings are verified, photos are real, and tenants contact landlords directly.

Tenants can search by area and budget, browse trending neighbourhoods and property types, and read what other tenants say. Owners get their own path to list a property.

Highlights

  • Search by location, property type and budget
  • Verified listings with real photos
  • Direct contact with landlords, no middlemen
  • Trending neighbourhoods and property type browsing
  • Separate flow for property owners
